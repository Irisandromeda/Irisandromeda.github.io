Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Summary

MouseOnMac works on your Mac and does not record, store or send what you type, click or scroll. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The only data that leaves your Mac is what is needed to verify and restore your purchase, handled by Apple and our purchase provider RevenueCat.

Who we are

MouseOnMac is developed and published by Olha Dyka, Ukraine ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the processing described in this policy.

Contact: innova.lab@ukr.net

Data that stays on your Mac

The following is processed only on your Mac. We cannot see or access it.

  • Mouse and keyboard input. With your permission (Accessibility and Input Monitoring), the app processes input events in real time to provide smooth scrolling, button actions and keyboard shortcuts. Events are handled in memory and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Connected devices. The app reads basic information about connected mice (such as name and type) to show them in settings.
  • Settings and shortcuts. Your preferences, button assignments and shortcuts are stored locally.
  • Paste text actions. Text you save for a paste action is stored locally and used only when you trigger that action. The app temporarily uses the clipboard and then restores its previous contents.
  • Free trial date. The start date of your free trial is stored in your Mac's Keychain and in your iCloud key-value storage, so the trial works consistently across reinstalls and your Macs. iCloud is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it.
  • Notifications. Reminders about your trial are scheduled locally on your Mac.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information.

To verify and restore your Lifetime purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app;
  • purchase and transaction details from Apple (such as product, date, price, currency and storefront country);
  • technical information (such as app version, macOS version, device model and language) and your IP address as part of the network connection.

This data is used only to provide, verify and restore purchases and to prevent fraud. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Legal bases and transfers

We process personal data in line with the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection". If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, the GDPR also applies: we process purchase data to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to prevent fraud and misuse of the free trial based on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data retention

Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide and restore your purchase and to meet legal obligations. Settings stay on your Mac until you delete the app. The trial start date may remain in your Keychain and iCloud after you delete the app, so the trial cannot be restarted; you can ask us how to remove it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict its processing, or object to it. Email innova.lab@ukr.net. Because we do not know your identity, please include your App Store order ID so we can find your purchase record.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example in your country of residence.

Children

MouseOnMac is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
