Support

We're happy to help with MouseOnMac.

Email: innova.lab@ukr.net

To help us answer faster, please include your macOS version, the MouseOnMac version (menu bar → About) and your mouse model.

The app says it needs permission

MouseOnMac needs Accessibility and Input Monitoring to work. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, turn on MouseOnMac under both Accessibility and Input Monitoring, then return to the app. If it's already on but the app still asks, switch it off and on again, or restart MouseOnMac.

Restore my purchase

Open MouseOnMac and choose "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Lifetime.

Smooth scrolling or a button doesn't work

Other mouse utilities (for example, Logitech Options+ or BetterTouchTool) can intercept the same events. Try quitting them and test again. Also check that the app you're using isn't in MouseOnMac's exclusion list.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
